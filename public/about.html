About

Prime Skills was established over 20 years ago by Nadeem Rashid. We help learners at all levels; whether you are learning to drive for the first time or training to become a driving instructor, lessons focus on essential driving skills with a focus on safety, clear instruction, and real confidence on the road.

Our mission is to give students the confidence to learn valuable life-long skills in a supportive, enjoyable environment, tailored to each individual's pace and needs.
