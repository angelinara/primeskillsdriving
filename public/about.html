Prime Skills was established in 2008 by Nadeem Rashid. As a former Catering Manager at Eton College, and after 20 years in that field, Nadeem qualified as a driving instructor in 2007. His passion for teaching and personable approach to helping students led to the creation of Prime Skills Academy.

At Prime Skills, our mission is to give students confidence in learning valuable life skills—whether they are new drivers or training to become professional driving instructors.

The Prime Skills levels of driving are: Beginner, Advanced, Professional, and Instructor, with courses for each stage, all with a strong focus on safe and responsible driving.

Lesson plans are designed to be enjoyable, supportive, and tailored to each student's pace and needs.
