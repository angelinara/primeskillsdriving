Driving Instructor Training

Set your own working hours, teach a valuable life skill, and qualify as a DVSA-approved driving instructor (ADI).

  • 1. Check eligibility
    Be aged 21+, hold a full UK driving licence for at least 3 years, have no more than 6 penalty points, and no driving ban in the last 4 years.
    Check eligibility on GOV.UK
  • 2. Complete a DBS check
    Required to become an approved driving instructor (ADI).
    Apply for a DBS check
  • 3. Prepare for ADI Part 1
    Practise multiple-choice questions and hazard perception.
    What's in the ADI Part 1 test
  • 4. Pass ADI Part 1 - Theory Test
    You must score at least 85% on the multiple choice questions and 57 out of 75 on the hazard perception test to pass.
    Book Part 1
  • 5. Begin instructor training for ADI Part 2 - Practical Test
    Start professional instructor training with Prime Skills.
    Enquire about training
  • 6. Pass ADI Part 2 - Driving Ability Test
    Assesses your advanced driving skills and safety standards.
    Book Part 2
  • 7. Complete compulsory training for Part 3
    Includes a minimum of 60 hours of instructor training to prepare for teaching.
  • 8. Pass ADI Part 3 - Teaching Ability Test
    A live lesson assessed by a DVSA examiner.
    Book Part 3
  • 9. Register with the DVSA
    Apply for your Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) badge and start teaching.
    Apply for your ADI badge

Common questions

Am I too late to change career?

No — many instructors start later in life. Commitment matters more than age.

Do I need teaching experience?

No. Teaching skills are part of the training.

Which areas do you cover?

Training covers: Maidenhead, Windsor, Eton, Slough, Henley, Cookham, Beaconsfield, Ascot, Burnham and Taplow.
