# Third-party assets

## Favicon

`public/favicon.png` and `public/favicon.ico` are rasterised from the car
emoji (U+1F699) in **Noto Color Emoji** by Google, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1.

<https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji>

The OFL states that "the requirement for fonts to remain under this license
does not apply to any document created using the Font Software", so an image
rendered from the font carries no licence obligation. This note is recorded
for provenance rather than out of necessity.
